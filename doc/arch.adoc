I would call the consensus mechanism something like Proof of Productive Activity (PoPA).

The concept is:

Instead of spending electricity to calculate meaningless hashes, the network’s economic value originates from verified productive activity.

A farmer plants maize. The platform records the farm, crop, location, planting cycle, estimated yield, and eventually the harvested quantity. Your existing AI components can contribute predictions and analytics. Verification mechanisms then determine whether the activity is genuine. Your valuation engine calculates the economic value, and the system issues a corresponding digital credit.

However, I would not directly claim that this creates a cryptocurrency with guaranteed monetary value. The digital asset needs a defined economic model. For example:

Verified agricultural activity → Agricultural Credit

Agricultural Credit → ecosystem utility

Actual crop sale → real revenue

Revenue → settlement or redemption

That creates a much stronger model than simply minting tokens.

The credit should have clearly defined utility. It could be used to pay for services inside the ecosystem, access agricultural financing, purchase farm inputs, pay marketplace fees, or participate in the network. Actual commodity sales can provide the underlying economic revenue.
